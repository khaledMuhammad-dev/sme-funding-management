Hover the control, not the glyph

The icon binds its hover state to the nearest interactive ancestor — a button, link, menu item, tab, or any element marked data-morph-host. Pointing at the 20px glyph is not the interaction; pressing Tab works too, because keyboard focus drives the same morph.

1 · Inside a button

Hover anywhere on the button — including the label — or Tab through them.

2 · Inside a nav link

The whole row is the target, exactly as in the admin sidebar.
